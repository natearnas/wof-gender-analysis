================================================================================
WHEEL OF FORTUNE LONGITUDINAL ANALYSIS - DIFFERENCE-IN-DIFFERENCES (DiD) RESULTS
6-SEASON DATASET (S36-S41, Sept 2018 - Sept 2023)
================================================================================
Analysis Date: December 13, 2025
Dataset: data/processed/longitudinal_data_raw.csv
Sample Size: 6,371 player records across 1,170 episodes
Regression Method: OLS (Ordinary Least Squares)
Dependent Variable: Player Winnings ($)

================================================================================
RESEARCH QUESTION
================================================================================

Does the introduction of the plastic cap in Season 38-39 (White Thing Era)
alter the gender dynamics of winnings in Wheel of Fortune?

Hypothesis: If the plastic cap provides an interface advantage to certain
cognitive styles, we should observe a change in the male-female winnings
gap before vs. after its introduction.

================================================================================
RESEARCH DESIGN
================================================================================

Treatment Group:  Seasons 38-39 (Plastic Cap Era)
Control Group:    Seasons 36-37, 40-41 (Pre/Post Plastic Cap)
Identification:   Parallel trends assumption + season/gender fixed effects

Model Specification:
  Winnings ~ Gender + Treatment + Gender×Treatment + Intercept

================================================================================
SAMPLE COMPOSITION
================================================================================

Season 36 (Sept 2018 - Sept 2018):  195 episodes
Season 37 (Sept 2018 - Sept 2019):  195 episodes
Season 38 (Sept 2019 - Sept 2020):  195 episodes (TREATMENT)
Season 39 (Sept 2020 - Sept 2021):  195 episodes (TREATMENT)
Season 40 (Sept 2021 - Sept 2022):  195 episodes
Season 41 (Sept 2022 - Sept 2023):  195 episodes

Total Episodes: 1,170
Total Player Records: 6,371

================================================================================
INTERPRETATION OF COEFFICIENTS
================================================================================

1. Intercept (β₀ = $6,709.86):
   Baseline winnings for female contestants in pre-treatment era (S36-S37).

2. Gender Effect (β₁ = $1,292.95, p<0.001):
   Male advantage in pre-treatment era: Men earned ~$1,293 MORE than women
   before the plastic cap era.

3. Treatment Effect (β₂ = $372.73, p=0.213):
   Overall effect of plastic cap era on female contestants: +$373
   (not statistically significant, women's winnings slightly higher in treatment period)

4. INTERACTION EFFECT (β₃ = -$1,274.59, p=0.006) **KEY FINDING**:
   The male advantage DECREASED by ~$1,275 during the plastic cap era.
   
   Interpretation:
   - Pre-treatment gender gap: $1,293 (men ahead)
   - Post-treatment gender gap: $1,293 - $1,275 = $18 (virtually no gap)
   
   The plastic cap interface change substantially NARROWED the gender gap,
   and the effect is statistically significant (p=0.006).

================================================================================
FINAL CONCLUSION
================================================================================

PRIMARY FINDING:
The plastic cap interface change (S38-S39) significantly altered gender
dynamics in Wheel of Fortune. The introduction of the plastic cap reduced
the male advantage in winnings by approximately $1,275 per player.

EFFECT SIZE:
- Pre-cap gender gap: ~$1,293 (males ahead)
- Post-cap gender gap: ~$18 (nearly eliminated)
- Reduction: 98.6% of the gender gap was closed

STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The interaction term (Gender × Treatment) is significant at p=0.006,
indicating this is not due to random variation.

INTERPRETATION:
The plastic cap appears to have leveled the playing field between genders,
suggesting that either:
  1. The old wheel interface advantaged male contestants
  2. The plastic cap reduced some advantage males possessed
  3. Female contestants adapted more effectively to the new interface

================================================================================
LIMITATIONS & CAVEATS
================================================================================

1. Sample Composition: Data from Andy's blog may be biased toward notable
   games (high winnings, unusual outcomes)

2. Missing Data: ~22% of episodes are not captured (data collection issues)

3. Confounding: Other changes may have occurred during S38-S39
   (e.g., contestant pool, rule changes, episode selection)

4. Parallel Trends: This analysis assumes pre-treatment trends would continue
   unchanged. Manual inspection of S36-S37 trends vs. S40-S41 would strengthen
   the analysis.

5. Low R²: The model explains only 0.3% of variance, suggesting gender and
   treatment account for a small fraction of winnings variation.

================================================================================
RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FURTHER RESEARCH
================================================================================

1. Collect complete episode data (not just from blog)
2. Control for contestant-level variables (e.g., demographics, game type)
3. Test for parallel trends assumption visually
4. Conduct placebo tests (false treatment dates)
5. Investigate mechanism: Why did the plastic cap affect gender gap?
   (e.g., fine motor skills, risk preference, experience distribution)
