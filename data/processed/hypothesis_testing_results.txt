FINAL CONCLUSION
================================================================================

PRIMARY FINDING: Male contestants win significantly more money than female
contestants in Wheel of Fortune, with a robust difference of approximately
$823 per player (95% CI: $377–$1,276).

EFFECT SIZE: The difference is both statistically significant (p=0.0126) and
economically substantial (~12% higher for men).

CONFIDENCE: High. Results are robust across:
  1. Parametric tests (T-test: p=0.0003)
  2. Non-parametric tests (Mann-Whitney: p=0.0126)
  3. Bootstrap simulation (CI excludes zero)

CAVEAT: This is a cross-sectional analysis. Gender may be confounded with
other factors (e.g., contestant demographics, time period effects). The
longitudinal (DiD) analysis addresses some of these concerns.
